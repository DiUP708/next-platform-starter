# Data Processing Agreement — saīsināts paraugs

> ⚠️ Šis ir **paraugs / starting point**, NE juridiska konsultācija. Pacientu (veselības) dati
> ir jutīgi. Pirms lielu deal parakstīšanas dod review juristam savā tirgū (US: HIPAA BAA var
> būt vajadzīgs; UK/EU: GDPR DPA). Mazākiem dabū vismaz parakstītu rakstisku vienošanos.

## DATA PROCESSING AGREEMENT

**Between:** [Clinic Name] ("Controller") and [Your Business Name] ("Processor")
**Effective date:** [date]

### 1. Subject matter
The Processor reactivates the Controller's dormant patients via SMS and email recall
campaigns on the Controller's behalf.

### 2. Roles
The Controller determines the purposes and means of processing. The Processor acts only on
the Controller's documented instructions.

### 3. Data processed
- Patient contact data: name, phone, email
- Visit metadata: last visit date, last treatment type
- No clinical/diagnostic records are required or to be shared.

### 4. Processor obligations
- Process personal data only on documented instructions from the Controller.
- Ensure persons authorised to process the data are bound by confidentiality.
- Implement appropriate technical and organisational security (encryption at rest and in
  transit, access controls, minimal retention).
- Assist the Controller with data-subject requests and opt-outs.
- Notify the Controller without undue delay of any personal data breach.
- Delete or return all personal data at the end of the engagement.

### 5. Patient rights & opt-out
Every message includes a clear opt-out (STOP / unsubscribe). Opt-outs are honoured
immediately and suppressed from all future campaigns.

### 6. Sub-processors
The Processor may use sub-processors (e.g. SMS/email/AI providers) under equivalent data
protection terms. List: [Twilio, Resend/SendGrid, Anthropic, n8n host].

### 7. Compliance
Both parties comply with applicable law: [GDPR & PECR (UK/EU)] / [HIPAA, TCPA, CAN-SPAM (US)].

### 8. Term & deletion
This agreement lasts for the engagement term. On termination, the Processor deletes all
patient data within [30] days and confirms deletion in writing.
