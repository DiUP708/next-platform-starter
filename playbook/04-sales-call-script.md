# 04 — Pārdošanas zvana skripts (15 min)

Struktūra: **Rapport → Discovery → Pitch → Garantija → Close → Onboarding**.
Mērķis: aiziet no zvana ar parakstītu "jā" un saplānotu onboarding.

---

## 1. Atvēršana (1 min)
```
"Thanks for jumping on, [Name]. I'll keep this to 15 minutes and be straight with you —
by the end you'll either want to try it or you won't, and either is totally fine.
Sound good?"
```

## 2. Discovery (4 min) — liec VIŅIEM atklāt sāpi
Jautā un KLAUSIES (pieraksti atbildes):
- "Roughly how many patient records do you have in total?"
- "And honestly, how many of those haven't been in for 12+ months?"
- "What's a typical first visit worth to the practice — ballpark?"
- "Do you currently do anything to win those lapsed patients back? How's that going?"
- "If 30–40 of them rebooked next month, what would that mean for you?"

> Tagad tev ir VIŅU skaitļi. Izmanto tos pitchā.

## 3. Pitch (3 min) — atkārto viņu skaitļus
```
"So you've got ~[their number] lapsed patients, each worth ~$[their value].
Even at a conservative 8% reactivation, that's [do the math] in revenue
that's just sitting there.

Here's what we do: we securely take that dormant list, and our AI sends warm,
personal texts and emails — in YOUR practice's voice — inviting them back.
It answers their questions and books them straight into your calendar, 24/7.
Your front desk does nothing except see a fuller schedule.

You approve every message before it goes out."
```

## 4. Garantija + cena (2 min) — noņem visu risku
```
"Now the part you'll like: there's no setup fee and no contract. You pay a flat
[$50–80] only for patients who book AND actually show up.

And if we don't rebook at least 15 patients in the first 30 days, you pay nothing.
The risk is entirely on me — I only win when your chairs get filled."
```

## 5. Close (2 min) — pieņēmuma close
```
"The only thing I'd need to get started is a quick export of your inactive patients
and about 20 minutes to set the messaging in your voice. We can be live within a week.

Should we get the paperwork over to you today so you don't miss next month's cycle?"
```

### Iebildumu apstrāde
- **"Send me info."** → "Happy to — but 5 minutes live beats a PDF. What's the one thing
  you'd need to see to feel good about a no-risk trial?"
- **"Is it compliant / data safe?"** → "Yes — we sign a data processing agreement, you stay
  the data controller, every message has an opt-out, and we follow [GDPR/HIPAA] practices."
- **"We're too busy."** → "That's exactly why this fits — it's 100% done-for-you and adds
  zero work for your team. Busy is good; let's fill the gaps you can't get to."
- **"Need to ask my partner."** → "Totally. Let's pencil the start for [date] contingent on
  their yes, so you keep the slot. I'll send a one-pager you can forward."

## 6. Onboarding (uzreiz pēc "jā")
1. Sūti DPA + vienkāršu service agreement (`templates/`).
2. Sūti onboarding checklist (`templates/onboarding-checklist.md`) → patient export.
3. Saplāno 20-min setup zvanu balsij/piedāvājumam.
4. Solījuma datums go-live: ≤7 dienas.

> **Mērķa konversija:** no kvalificēta zvana → 20–35% paraksta, kad piedāvājums ir nulles riska.
> 10 zvani ≈ 2–3 klienti. Tāpēc outreach apjoms nedēļā 1–2 ir viss.
